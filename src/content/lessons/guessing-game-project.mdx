Pick a secret number. The player guesses until they get it right — classic **`while`** + **`if`** + **`break`** practice.

On your computer, replace the list with **`while True:`** and **`int(input(...))`**.

## Full while loop pattern (local Python)

```python
secret = 7
while True:
    guess = int(input("Guess: "))
    if guess == secret:
        print("You win!")
        break
    elif guess < secret:
        print("Too low")
    else:
        print("Too high")
```

## Stretch goals

- Count guesses with a counter
- Limit to 5 tries, then `break` with "Game over"

## Recap

- **`while`** repeats until **`break`**
- Compare guess to secret with **`==`**, **`<`**, **`>`**
