## f-strings (recommended)

<PythonExample
  filename="fstring.py"
  code={`name = "Ada"
age = 36
print(f"My name is {name} and I am {age} years old.")
print(f"Next year: {age + 1}")`}
/>

## Expressions inside {}

<PythonExample
  filename="expr.py"
  code={`price = 19.99
qty = 3
total = price * qty
print(f"Total: {total:.2f}")`}
/>

The `:.2f` inside braces formats a number to 2 decimal places.

## format() method

<PythonExample
  filename="format.py"
  code={`print("Hello, {}!".format("Python"))
print("{0} + {1} = {2}".format(2, 3, 5))`}
/>

## % formatting (older style)

<PythonExample
  filename="percent.py"
  code={`name = "Sam"
print("Hello, %s" % name)`}
/>

You may see this in old code; prefer f-strings for new projects.

<Callout variant="tip">
  f-strings are readable, fast, and support expressions — use them by default.
</Callout>
