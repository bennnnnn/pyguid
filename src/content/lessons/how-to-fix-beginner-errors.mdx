## Why this matters

Panic fades when you substitute **routine steps** for vibes. Memorize one beginner checklist once; reuse it forever.

## Beginner troubleshooting checklist

1. **Final line**: what **exception** (`SyntaxError`, `NameError`, `TypeError`, …)?
2. **Quoted line**: do **`"`**, **`'`**, **`(`**, **`)`** all pair?
3. **Spelling**: is every name identical character-for-character everywhere?
4. **Case**: is it **`print`**, not **`Print`**?
5. **Order**: assign variables **before** `print(variable)` reads them
6. **Types (preview)**: mixing strings and numbers intentionally?

## Recap

- **One change per rerun** avoids mystery compounding bugs
- **Syntax** → imbalance / illegal symbols; **Name** → undefined or typo/case mix-up; **Type** → mismatched operands (preview tier)
- Return to **`print("Runner OK")`** when suspicion lands on tooling instead of typing
