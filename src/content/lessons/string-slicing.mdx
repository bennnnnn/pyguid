**Slicing** takes a substring with `[start:end]` — `end` is not included:

<PythonExample
  filename="slice.py"
  code={`text = "Python"
print(text[0:3])
print(text[2:])
print(text[:4])`}
/>

## Negative indexes in slices

<PythonExample
  filename="neg_slice.py"
  code={`text = "Programming"
print(text[-3:])
print(text[:-1])`}
/>

## Step in slices

`[start:end:step]`:

<PythonExample
  filename="step.py"
  code={`text = "abcdef"
print(text[::2])
print(text[::-1])`}
/>

## in, find, replace

<PythonExample
  filename="methods.py"
  code={`sentence = "Python is fun"
print("fun" in sentence)
print(sentence.find("is"))
print(sentence.replace("fun", "great"))`}
/>

## split and join

<PythonExample
  filename="split_join.py"
  code={`csv_line = "ada,36,london"
parts = csv_line.split(",")
print(parts)

joined = "-".join(["2024", "05", "23"])
print(joined)`}
/>

<Callout variant="tip">
  `split()` without arguments splits on any whitespace — useful for sentences.
</Callout>
