<h2 id="integers-floats">Integers and floats</h2>

Whole numbers are `int`. Numbers with decimals are `float`.

<PythonExample
  filename="numbers.py"
  code={`count = 3
price = 9.99
print(count)
print(price)`}
/>

<h2 id="strings">Strings and quotes</h2>

Text lives in strings. Use single or double quotes.

<PythonExample
  filename="strings.py"
  code={`greeting = "Hello"
name = 'Python'
print(greeting, name)`}
/>

<h2 id="booleans">Booleans</h2>

`True` and `False` represent yes/no values.

<PythonExample
  filename="bools.py"
  code={`is_active = True
print(is_active)
print(not is_active)`}
/>

<h2 id="type-function">Checking types with type()</h2>

Use `type()` to see what kind of value you have.

<PythonExample
  filename="types.py"
  code={`x = 42
print(type(x))
y = "hello"
print(type(y))`}
/>
